# 01 – Business & Domain Understanding

## Overview

This folder contains the initial research and planning for my individual **AI Car Damage Detection** project, developed as a potential solution for the Bulgarian insurance company Bul Ins.

The goal is to explore how machine learning can support motor insurance claims handlers by automatically classifying visible vehicle damage from photographs, reducing repetitive manual work and supporting the initial assessment of claims.

Before developing the models, I investigated the insurance domain, gathered stakeholder input, defined the project scope, and established the data requirements.

## Activities & Evidence

### 1. [AI Project Proposal](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/AI-Car-Damage-Detection/blob/main/01%20%E2%80%93%20Business%20%26%20Domain%20Understanding/AI_Project_Proposal.pdf)

Defined the business problem, project objectives, stakeholders, research question, and proposed machine learning approach. The proposal outlines the development stages, from initial classification models to a potential working prototype.

### 2. [Domain Understanding – Bul Ins](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/AI-Car-Damage-Detection/blob/main/01%20%E2%80%93%20Business%20%26%20Domain%20Understanding/Domain_Understanding_BulIns_Expanded(2).pdf)

Researched the Bulgarian motor insurance industry and Bul Ins's claims environment, including current processes, stakeholder needs, potential business value, and technical challenges.

I also investigated legal, ethical, privacy, and environmental considerations, including GDPR, the EU AI Act, and the limitations of using AI in insurance decision-making.

### 3. [Claims Process Interview Questions](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/AI-Car-Damage-Detection/blob/main/01%20%E2%80%93%20Business%20%26%20Domain%20Understanding/claims_process_interview_questions.pdf)

Prepared questions for an interview with Bul Ins to better understand its current claims-handling process, identify repetitive manual activities, and explore opportunities for automation.

The interview highlighted manual data entry, document handling, and repeated information checks. However, the specific value of automated photo classification still requires further validation with stakeholders.

### 4. [Data Requirements](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/AI-Car-Damage-Detection/blob/main/01%20%E2%80%93%20Business%20%26%20Domain%20Understanding/Data_Requirements.pdf)

Defined the data needed to develop a supervised image-classification model, including image formats, damage labels, annotation requirements, dataset quality, class representation, and legal considerations.

I also established dataset selection criteria and identified potential challenges involving class imbalance, image diversity, and data preparation.

## Outcome

This phase helped me translate a real business problem into a machine learning classification task.

Through domain research, stakeholder interviews, and data requirements analysis, I established the project's initial scope and identified the challenges that need to be addressed during model development.

The research also highlighted the importance of **data quality, stakeholder validation, responsible AI, and human oversight** when developing machine learning solutions for the insurance industry.
